# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Engineers ensure product quality by developing test strategies, executing tests, and validating that features meet acceptance criteria. They collaborate closely with developers and product managers to identify and triage defects early.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain comprehensive test cases and test plans
- Conduct manual and automated testing across features and platforms
- Verify acceptance criteria and requirements are met before release
- Collaborate with developers to triage bugs and reproduce issues
- Identify gaps in test coverage and recommend improvements
- Document test results and maintain defect tracking systems
- Participate in sprint planning to understand testing scope and risks

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reliability before release
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Build confidence in product stability through comprehensive test coverage
- Enable fast feedback loops between development and testing

### Typical Communication
- Test case documentation and coverage reports
- Daily standups and sprint planning sessions
- Bug triage meetings with developers and PMs
- Quality status updates and release readiness assessments
- Defect summaries in sprint retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, reproduce issues, discuss technical testing challenges
- **Product Managers**: Review acceptance criteria, discuss quality expectations and trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Report on test progress, quality blockers, and release readiness
- **Stakeholders**: Provide quality assurance reports and release readiness summaries

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive, usable, and aesthetically pleasing user experiences. They bridge user needs with technical implementation, ensuring products are both functional and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather feedback to inform design decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs for features
- Establish and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Collaborate with product managers to refine requirements and user flows
- Work with developers to ensure designs are feasible and well-implemented
- Test designs with users and iterate based on feedback
- Document design specifications and handoff to development teams
- Ensure accessibility standards are met across all designs

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and product adoption
- Reduce user confusion and support requests through clear design
- Create consistent, on-brand experiences across all touchpoints
- Enable faster development through clear design specifications

### Typical Communication
- Design mockups, wireframes, and interactive prototypes
- Design specifications and component documentation
- User research findings and insights
- Design review meetings with developers and product managers
- Feedback and iteration cycles based on user testing

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on requirements, user stories, and success metrics
- **Developers**: Provide design specifications, discuss implementation feasibility, collaborate on component development
- **QA Engineers**: Review designs for testability and usability flows
- **Stakeholders**: Present design concepts, gather feedback, demonstrate user-centered approach

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain the infrastructure, tooling, and automation that enable reliable, scalable deployments. They enable developers to ship code safely and project managers to meet release schedules.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments (staging, production), and deployment processes
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems for observability
- Optimize deployment reliability, speed, and rollback capabilities
- Collaborate with developers on release readiness and performance optimization
- Manage infrastructure costs and resource utilization
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks for incident response
- Coordinate with project managers on deployment windows and schedules

### Goals
- Enable safe, rapid, repeatable deployments to production
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Reduce time-to-market through automation and efficiency
- Provide visibility into system health and performance

### Typical Communication
- Deployment pipelines and release documentation
- Infrastructure and environment status reports
- Performance metrics and monitoring dashboards
- Incident postmortems and deployment reviews
- Collaboration with developers on CI/CD optimization

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support development environment setup, review code for deployment readiness, collaborate on performance optimization
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment schedules, report on infrastructure readiness, support release planning
- **QA Engineers**: Provide test environments, support automated test execution in pipelines
- **Stakeholders**: Report on system reliability and uptime metrics

---

## Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent business interests, user needs, and organizational priorities. They provide requirements, feedback, and approvals that guide project direction and ensure alignment with broader business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria for projects
- Provide feedback and approval on features, designs, and roadmap
- Attend demos and reviews to validate deliverables meet expectations
- Escalate priorities and resolve conflicts between competing interests
- Communicate project status and outcomes to broader organization
- Provide subject matter expertise (SME) as needed
- Support resource allocation and removal of organizational blockers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between product delivery and strategic priorities
- Enable cross-functional teams to operate with clear direction and authority
- Reduce rework and scope creep through upfront clarity

### Typical Communication
- Project charters and one-pagers for approval
- Weekly or bi-weekly status updates and progress reports
- Feature demos and acceptance reviews
- Priority discussions and trade-off conversations
- Escalation and decision-making communications
- Announcement of project outcomes and lessons learned

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define requirements, provide feedback, discuss trade-offs and prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates, approve schedules and plans, remove blockers
- **Developers & QA**: Attend demos, provide feature feedback, clarify requirements
- **Designers**: Review design concepts, provide user/business perspective on experience
