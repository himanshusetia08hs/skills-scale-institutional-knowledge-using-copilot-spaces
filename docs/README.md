## About OctoAcme Project Management

OctoAcme delivers projects using clear ownership, measurable goals, and iterative improvement. We follow a structured **five-phase lifecycle**: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective. Our approach emphasizes:

- **Customer-first thinking**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative, incremental delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments rather than large monolithic releases
- **Clear roles and ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Manager with defined responsibilities
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Transparent risk management**: Identify, assess, and communicate risks openly with structured escalation paths
- **Cross-functional communication**: Maintain consistent cadences (daily standups, weekly syncs, stakeholder updates) to ensure alignment

### Key Principles

Our core roles include the **Project Manager** (coordinates delivery, schedules, risks), **Product Manager** (defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog), **Developers** (implement features), **QA/Testing** (validates quality), and **Stakeholders** (provide inputs and approvals). All projects use a central source of truth—typically GitHub Projects—to track work through defined workflow stages: Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done.

### Quality & Continuous Improvement

Quality is embedded throughout execution via unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end smoke tests supported by automated CI/CD pipelines with security scanning. Risk management is systematic using a Risk Register reviewed weekly, and continuous improvement is formalized through retrospectives after each sprint or milestone where teams capture learnings and prioritize 2–3 action items for iterative enhancement.
